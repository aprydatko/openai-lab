# Logistics Operations Policy

Drivers must report any incident to dispatch within 15 minutes of becoming aware of it.

Dispatch should notify the safety manager immediately when hazardous cargo is delayed, rerouted, or held at a checkpoint.

Temperature-controlled cargo must remain between 2C and 8C during transport, and any deviation longer than 10 minutes must be escalated.

If a truck misses a delivery window, dispatch should contact the receiver, update the ETA in the system, and document the reason for the delay.

Proof of delivery should be uploaded before the end of the driver's shift whenever network coverage is available.

# Incident Note

At 08:40 local time, a truck carrying paint solvent was delayed after a tire issue on the ring road near the east warehouse.

The driver reported no injuries and no cargo leak, but the vehicle is not safe to continue until roadside assistance arrives.

The shipment is now projected to miss its customer delivery window by 2 hours.

Dispatch has already informed the customer success team, but the consignee has not yet received a revised ETA.

Weather conditions are stable. A replacement vehicle is available at the depot, but no transfer decision has been made yet.
